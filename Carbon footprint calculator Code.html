    Our Mission

    Carbon literacy for every community

    EcoStep is a student-built community engagement project with one simple belief: when people can see their environmental impact, they act on it.

    Why we built this

    Most people genuinely want to live more sustainably — but "reduce your carbon footprint" is vague advice when you don't know what your footprint is, or which habits actually matter. Professional carbon audits are expensive and aimed at corporations, while most online calculators bury users in jargon.

    EcoStep was created as a 2nd-year Information Technology community engagement project to close that gap: a free, friendly tool that any household, student group, or local small business can use in under three minutes — no sign-up, no data collection, no jargon.

    What we stand for

    🔍

    Transparency

    Every calculation uses published emission factors, and the math is explained in the open-source code. No black boxes.

    🔒

    Privacy

    Your answers never leave your device. Results are saved only in your own browser's local storage — delete them anytime.

    🎯

    Actionability

    A number without a plan changes nothing. Our Reduction Advisor always pairs your result with concrete next steps.

    🤝

    Community

    Individual change matters most when it spreads. EcoStep is designed to be shared — in classrooms, housing societies, and local businesses.

    How the estimates work

    EcoStep converts your everyday inputs into annual kilograms of CO₂ using simplified, publicly available emission factors adapted for the Indian context, for example:

    • 🚗 0.19 kg CO₂ per km in an average petrol car; 0.05 kg per km on a bike/scooter
    • 🚌 0.08 kg CO₂ per passenger-km on public transport (bus / metro / train)
    • 🚶 0 kg CO₂ for walking — every km walked instead of driven avoids ~0.19 kg
    • ⚡ 0.82 kg CO₂ per unit (kWh) of grid electricity, estimated from your bill at ~₹8/unit
    • 🔥 ~42 kg CO₂ per 14.2 kg LPG cylinder (≈ 0.05 kg per ₹ spent on gas)
    • 🍽️ Food footprint: vegetarian ≈ 1,100 kg/yr; non-vegetarian ≈ 1,700 kg/yr
    • 🌳 One mature tree absorbs about 22 kg CO₂ per year

    These are educational estimates, not an official carbon audit — but they're accurate enough to show you where your biggest opportunities are.
